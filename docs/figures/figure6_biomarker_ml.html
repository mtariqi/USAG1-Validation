Clinical relevance: This 11-gene salivary signature detects BMP pathway activation with AUC = 0.89 — providing a non-invasive pharmacodynamic readout for TRG035 Phase II trials, enabling dose-response assessment months before visible tooth formation appears on imaging.

SHAP analysis identifies SMAD1 (mean |SHAP| = 0.31), ID1 (0.28), and BMP4 (0.24) as the top three features. The signature is biologically coherent: BMP target genes (ID1, ID3, SMAD1), pathway feedback regulators (BAMBI, GREM1, FST, TWSG1, CHRD), RUNX2 (odontoblast master TF), and SOSTDC1 itself (direct target engagement marker).
