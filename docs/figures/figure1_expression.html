Secretory fibroblasts (gingiva, mean log-CPM = 3.8; 72% detection) and distal tubule cells (kidney, 3.5; 68%) show the highest expression, consistent with USAG-1's secretory extracellular function. Odontoblasts (dental pulp, 2.6; 52%) and pulp fibroblasts (2.9; 61%) confirm target expression in the cells directly responsible for dentin formation.
